Claims FAQ

How fast do you respond?+

Emergency medical cases receive callbacks in under 5 minutes. Property and motor claims receive status updates within 2 working hours.

Can I track my claim online?+

Yes, use the tracking link provided in your acknowledgment email or log into the agency portal.

Which documents are required?+

Typical requirements include ID, policy schedule, police or medical reports and photos of the incident.

Document checklist

  • Copy of policy schedule or certificate
  • National ID or passport
  • Incident report (police, fire brigade, medical)
  • Photos or videos of damage
  • Repair estimates or hospital invoices
