About Aredo

Insurance born in Baghdad, built for Iraq’s future

Headquartered on Zaha Hadid Street, Aredo blends global-grade underwriting with local knowledge to protect people, companies and communities.

Our story

Founded by Iraqi insurance veterans, Aredo bridges the gap between international best practice and the specific needs of Baghdad, Basra, Mosul and beyond.

Mission

To deliver protection that powers progress for individuals, entrepreneurs and national projects.

Vision

To be Iraq’s most trusted insurer, recognized for transparency, innovation and impact.
