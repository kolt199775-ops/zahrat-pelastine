Who we are

About Bakeet

Bakeet is the lunch-box brand born from a collective of chefs who wanted to ship joy, not just calories. Every smile logo is hand sketched from the Arabic letter "ب" (Baa), a nod to our roots and our promise.
Mission: deliver curated global lunch boxes with warmth, precision, and sustainability. We source locally, tell stories digitally, and celebrate cultural mashups.
Why we are unique: liquid-glass packaging, chef-paired playlists, adaptive nutrition data, and a signature Bakeet Red ribbon that wraps every box.
The smile logo represents the exact curve we want to see on your face right after the first bite. Keep it on your desk, it glows softly in the dark.
